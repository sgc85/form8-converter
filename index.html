Checks and limitations

  • Uses the exact 12-page approved master PDF as the document backing. It does not recreate or redraw the form.
  • Rejects an original that does not have matching page sizes, PDF form field names, types, widget counts and positions.
  • Clears all prefilled master text and checkboxes before copying the originals, then opens the saved result again and verifies its field values.
  • Warns about missing Part 3 selections or declaration information and excessive narrative lengths. Field comparison cannot guarantee that long text prints without clipping.
  • Preserves editable PDF fields. Cryptographic signatures cannot be copied safely; any typed assessor name or signature copied from the old form must be checked.
  • Runs on your device using browser JavaScript, with no document upload, server API, third-party CDN or analytics. Original PDFs and outputs remain in the browser tab until removed or the tab is closed.
For internal prototype evaluation only. Do not put pupil files, completed PDFs or mapping documents in this public GitHub repository.
